# Workshop: Chartering Kickoff

**When:** Days 1–10 of Phase 1. **Duration:** half-day. **Attendees:** Executive Sponsor, Data Governance Manager, Cegeka Lead Advisor + Governance Architect.

## Objectives

1. Ratify the Hub charter's mandate and scope (what the central standards body does and doesn't own).
2. Agree voting mechanics for the Operational Governance Committee.
3. Identify candidate pilot Spoke domain(s).

## Agenda

| Time | Item |
|---|---|
| 0:00–0:15 | Why federated governance, not centralized or fully decentralized — the cost-of-poor-data-quality case (`../../offering/offering-overview.md`) |
| 0:15–0:45 | Walk through `../../governance-as-code/councils/hub-charter.yaml` field by field — mandate, operational scope, veto rights, voting mechanics, arbitration path |
| 0:45–1:10 | Decide and record: voting threshold, veto scope, escalation SLA |
| 1:10–1:30 | Select pilot Spoke domain(s); identify candidate Business Owner |
| 1:30–1:45 | Confirm committee meeting cadence and next session date |

## Output

- Hub charter YAML filled in and committed to the client's repo fork.
- Pilot domain(s) selected and named in writing.
- Date set for first Operational Committee session (target: Day 21–30).
